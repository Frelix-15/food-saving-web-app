    SaveBite keeps a running countdown on everything in your kitchen, flags what needs eating first, and turns those ingredients into dinner with AI.

    Your food list stays in this browser. Nothing leaves your device unless you use the AI features.

    About a third of the food the world produces is never eaten.

    That is the UN Food and Agriculture Organization's estimate. A lot of it goes off at the back of someone's fridge. SaveBite is a small habit that keeps yours from joining it.

    How it works

    1. 1

      Add what you bought

      Name, quantity, category and a date. If you are not sure of the date, SaveBite suggests a typical shelf life.

    2. 2

      Watch the tags

      Every item gets a live countdown. Anything close to its date turns orange, then red, and moves to the top.

    3. 3

      Cook what is expiring

      Pick the foods you want to use and get meal ideas built around them. Mark them used and your savings add up.

    Everything in one place

    • Countdown tags

      A live days-and-seconds countdown on every item, colour-coded by urgency.

    • AI meal ideas

      Choose what you have and get meals that use the soonest-expiring food first.

    • Recipe book

      Built-in recipes show which ingredients you already own and what is missing.

    • Photo recognition

      Snap your groceries and let AI name them and suggest how long they will last.

    • Reminders

      Desktop notifications and an in-app list before anything goes off.

    • Savings tracker

      Foods saved, money kept and waste-free streaks, week by week.
